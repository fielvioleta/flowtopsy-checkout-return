Flowtopsy is a Chrome extension. It records a browser session on your device and turns that session into a structured flow, a test case, a user manual, and Playwright or Cypress scaffolding. Recordings stay on your device.

PlanPriceWhat you get
Free $0 5 Start Recording uses for the life of a verified account. No card.
Pro monthly $12 / month Unlimited Start Recording, exports, and 30 AI manual polishes plus 30 AI test-case polishes each UTC month. Renews monthly until you cancel.
Pro yearly $99 / year Same as Pro monthly, billed once a year. Renews yearly until you cancel.

Included with Pro

  • Unlimited Start Recording
  • Markdown test case, Playwright, Cypress, user manual, and screenshot ZIP export
  • Optional AI polish of manual and test-case text (capped per month)

There is no team plan, cloud storage of recordings, or consulting service. You use the software yourself.

How billing works

Pro is sold as a subscription through Paddle, the merchant of record. You can cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for, unless a refund is issued under the refund policy.
